What the tool does

The tool is used only by NAARIX ENTERPRISE to read the performance reports of our own Amazon advertising campaigns and to manage our own campaigns (bids, keywords, budgets). It is not offered to any other company or person.

What data we access

Only data about our own seller and advertising account: campaign settings, keywords, bids, impressions, clicks, spend and sales figures. We do not request, collect or store personal information about Amazon customers.

How data is used and stored

The data is used only for our own sales and advertising decisions. It is stored on our business computer and in a private backup, protected by passwords, multi-factor authentication, a firewall and anti-malware software. Data is transferred over encrypted (HTTPS) connections only.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or give this data to any other company. Our own reports may be analysed with an AI assistant service that runs on our business computer; no customer personal information is included.
